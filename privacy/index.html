Privacy Policy (Chính sách quyền riêng tư)

Effective Date: November 12, 2026

1. Overview & Zero-Server Architecture

CloudMix ("the App") is a client-side mobile media organizer that enables users to view, back up, transfer, and manage their own photos and videos across their local Android device storage, their personal Google Drive account(s), and their personal Telegram cloud storage. CloudMix operates 100% locally on the user's device and does not operate any intermediary backend servers that collect, store, or inspect user files or credentials.

2. Google User Data Accessed

When you explicitly sign in with Google OAuth 2.0, CloudMix requests access to your Google Drive solely on your device in order to:

  • Display your Google Drive storage quota and connected account email address so you can distinguish multiple connected Drive accounts.
  • List, preview, stream, upload, organize (inside CloudMix_Vault), transfer, or delete photo and video files in your Google Drive strictly at your explicit command.

3. Data Storage, Security & No Third-Party Sharing

  • Local Storage Only: OAuth 2.0 access and refresh tokens are stored exclusively within the private application sandbox on your personal Android device.
  • Direct HTTPS Communication: All requests go directly from your device over TLS/HTTPS to official Google APIs (googleapis.com).
  • Zero Selling or Sharing: CloudMix never sells, rents, transfers, or shares Google user data with any third parties, advertisers, analytics brokers, or AI training pipelines.

4. Google API Services User Data Policy (Limited Use Disclosure)

CloudMix's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

5. Revoking Access & Deleting Data

You may disconnect any Google Drive account at any time with a single tap inside CloudMix Settings (which immediately purges the stored OAuth tokens from your device), or revoke access at any time from your Google Account Security Permissions page.

6. Tóm tắt Tiếng Việt

CloudMix hoạt động trực tiếp 100% trên điện thoại của bạn, không có máy chủ trung gian thu thập dữ liệu. Khi bạn bấm Đăng nhập Google Drive, khóa phiên OAuth và toàn bộ ảnh/video chỉ truyền trực tiếp giữa thiết bị của bạn và Google Drive chính chủ của bạn. Bạn có thể gỡ kết nối bất kỳ lúc nào ngay trong mục Cài đặt của ứng dụng.
